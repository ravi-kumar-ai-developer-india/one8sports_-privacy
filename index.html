1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Ravi Kumar Ai Developer India ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you use our mobile application One8Sports (package name: com.one8sports.app) available on Google Play.

We are deeply committed to respecting your privacy, protecting your personal data, and maintaining strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play User Data Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide our grassroots sports ecosystem, player matchmaking, tournament management, ground reservations, and AI skill assessment features:

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Profile: Name, phone number, email address, city/district, sports discipline (Cricket, Football, Badminton, Kabaddi, Hockey, Basketball, Volleyball), and competitive level.
  • User Role: Athlete, Coach, Parent, Ground Owner, or Tournament Organizer.
  • Athletic Profile Data: Performance tags, strike rates, match records, and sports benchmarks.

B. Authentication Credentials

  • Google Sign-In: Public profile (name, email, photo URL) securely processed via Firebase Authentication.
  • Phone & SMS OTP: Mobile number verification via one-time SMS codes for secure phone login.

C. User Generated & Activity Content

  • Tournament registrations, team rosters, and bracket fixtures.
  • Turf, court, and ground booking schedules.
  • Community posts, match updates, and scorecards.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To register, authenticate, and manage user accounts securely.
  • To compute AI Talent Scout (AIT) performance benchmarks and athlete stats.
  • To coordinate live match scores, tournament schedules, and brackets.
  • To manage ground and turf reservations with venue owners.
  • To connect verified coaches, scouts, and athletes within local sports communities.
  • To protect against fraud, spam, and bot activity.

4. No Third-Party Selling or Advertising

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party data brokers or advertising networks. We utilize only trusted Google Cloud infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (Google LLC): Cloud infrastructure utilizing Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) rules to securely store user profiles, scores, and bookings under HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Google Play Services: Core application updates and platform integrity verification.

5. Android Permissions Required

One8Sports requests only the minimum required Android runtime permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to sync match scores, ground bookings, and user profiles with Cloud Firestore.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects online/offline connection state.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: (Optional, Android 13+) Sends fixture alerts, match reminders, and booking updates.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Haptic feedback for button clicks.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard technical security practices:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and backend use Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Zero-Trust Database Rules: Database documents in /users/{userId} are restricted so only the authenticated owner can access their personal data.

7. User Rights & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users have complete control over their data:

  • In-App Reset: Clear cached athlete records and local history at any time via Settings > System > Zero Fake Data Reset.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: Users can request full permanent deletion of their account and all Firestore records at our web deletion page or by emailing aicreation79108@gmail.com. All data will be permanently erased within 48 hours.

8. Children's Privacy

One8Sports supports grassroots youth sports. Children under 13 years of age may only use the app under the supervision of a registered parent or legal guardian using our dedicated Parent / Guardian role. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental authorization.
